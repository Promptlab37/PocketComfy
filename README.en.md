# 🎬 PocketComfy by PromptLab

### A whole AI studio in your pocket, powered by your own computer

**Video · Images · Editing · Upscaling · Music · Voice · 3D**

An Android app that drives your own ComfyUI server from your phone.
No cloud, no subscription: the phone is a remote control for a computer with a graphics card.

## What the app does

- **Video**: from an image or from text, long video from chained parts, talking scene with voice
- **Images**: text to image, editing, outpainting, face swap, old photo restoration
- **Upscaling** of images and videos
- **Music** and **voice**
- **3D model** from a photo

The app needs your own computer running a ComfyUI server with a powerful graphics card.
